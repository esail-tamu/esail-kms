    Check Your Understanding¶

    Ten questions covering the naming convention from the training page. Answer one at a time — if you miss one, that choice is eliminated and you try again, so you can't move on until you get it right. Finishing the quiz means you've got the whole convention down, no grading needed.

    Questions are pulled from a bank and shuffled (wording, examples, and answer order), so what you see may differ from what someone else sees.

    Once you finish, your email and a quick attempts-per-question summary are sent to esail-subscriptions@tamu.edu via Formspree.
